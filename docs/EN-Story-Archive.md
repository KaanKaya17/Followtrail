# Story archive

[User guide](EN-Home.md) · [Privacy and data](EN-Privacy.md) · [Platform status](EN-Releases.md)

## Open the archive

On Windows, use the story archive action for your account or a saved profile. On Android, use the Overview options for your own account or **More → Story archive** on a saved profile. Opening or browsing the calendar uses local data. **Check stories**, followed by confirmation, reads currently available stories that your linked account can access. This is an on-demand check.

## Browse the calendar

Use the previous and next month buttons, then choose a day. Narrow screens open the day viewer. Wider screens can show the calendar and viewer together. Both platforms group days by UTC, which can differ from your local date near midnight.

When available, Tracker shows the saved media, time, mentions, links, locations, hashtags and shared posts. A mention can exist without a repost. Music and stickers may behave differently from Instagram's viewer. A failed media download can leave metadata only.

## Storage and limits

The encrypted local archive can hold up to 200 items and 256 MiB of media per account, with a 16 MiB per-file limit. Media downloads from the CDN do not include your session cookie. Retention and deletion settings apply to this archive. Story media is outside the scope of the current single-file backup.
